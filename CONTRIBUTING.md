# Contributing to Bruma

Thanks for helping. The terms of a contribution are Micelio's, shared by every repository, and live in
[`micelio-engineering`](https://github.com/bogd3v/micelio-engineering/blob/main/docs/contributing.md): the
Developer Certificate of Origin and the pull request template. The license of your contribution is this
repository's, `MIT OR Apache-2.0` ([LICENSE-MIT](LICENSE-MIT), [LICENSE-APACHE](LICENSE-APACHE)).

- Commands, structure and conventions are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); run its check command before opening
  the pull request.
- Third-party material goes in [THIRD-PARTY.md](THIRD-PARTY.md) and [REUSE.toml](REUSE.toml).
- Decisions are recorded in [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md).
